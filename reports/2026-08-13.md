## Selected opportunity and why it matters now

The fallback change adds a narrow regression test for `reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. The Telegram workflow depends on exactly one `telegram-brief` block with predictable field labels. A duplicated marker, missing field, or content before the brief can break extraction or cause unintended content to be sent. Testing this interface is useful immediately because the reporting pipeline is now part of the daily automation.

Expected impact: catch structural report regressions during normal test execution before they affect Telegram delivery. The test intentionally does not validate secrets or prose quality; it protects only the stable structural contract.
