## Selected opportunity and impact
The PennyRush parser's fallback currently treats a positive value in a single signed `Amount` column as an expense when no textual transaction-type signal is present. That can turn salary, refunds, and other incoming transactions into spending. The prepared fix preserves explicit text precedence, keeps negative values as expenses, and makes positive signed values infer income.
