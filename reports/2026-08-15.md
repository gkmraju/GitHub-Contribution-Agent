## Selected opportunity and why it matters now

PennyRush imports financial statements. In the affected single-Amount-column format, a positive amount such as salary can be categorized as an expense when no textual type signal exists. This can propagate incorrect values into spending totals and visualizations. The fix uses information the parser already has—the sign of the parsed amount—without changing schemas, dependencies, UI, auth, or privacy behavior.

## Implementation already completed

Branch: `fix/signed-amount-income` in `gkmraju/PennyRush`.

Changes:
1. `web/lib/parsers/csv.ts`: after explicit income/transfer/expense textual signals and negative-amount handling, a positive amount now falls back to `income` rather than unconditional `expense`.
2. `web/lib/parsers/csv.test.ts`: regression coverage parses a signed Amount CSV containing `-120.00` and `50000.00` and asserts `["expense", "income"]`.
