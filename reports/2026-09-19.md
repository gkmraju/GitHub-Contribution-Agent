## Selected change
Issue #2495 proposes the narrow DOM-readiness correction `observer.observe(document.body || document.documentElement, ...)`, including the second observer site, plus a longer content-wait ceiling supported by the reporter's verified local repair. This is a real reliability defect rather than cosmetic work: evaluating during the transient pre-body document currently throws before the adapter can detect content/login/security state.

Expected impact: prevent a deterministic `MutationObserver` TypeError during redirect-time evaluation and allow the observer to see `<body>` insertion through the document element.
