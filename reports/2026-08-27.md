## Selected opportunity

Upstream issue #3049 reports a reproducible correctness bug: a method-agnostic decorator route such as NestJS `@All` can collide with a same-URL filesystem route. Because wildcard route identity drops the method and filesystem routes are pre-seeded into `routeRegistry`, the decorator's resolved handler can be stored under the same URL-only key and later stamped onto the surviving filesystem Route node. The issue demonstrates that this creates a fabricated cross-framework handler attribution rather than merely omitting a route.

This matters because GitNexus route-impact analysis can then report the wrong handler/application in monorepos that combine filesystem routing with another framework at the same URL. The issue was opened by a collaborator and includes a concrete reproduction, controls, mechanism analysis, suggested fixes, and a missing regression-test tier.
